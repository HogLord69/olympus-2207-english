# Credits

This English edition is a thin layer over a great deal of other people's work.
All of it existed before this project did, and none of it was made for money.

Names are transliterated from the game's own credits file. Where someone is
known by a handle, the handle is kept.

## Olympus 2207 (Fixed Edition)

**Foxx.** The Fixed Edition is his rebuild — updated sfall, updated
SF-Configurator, mod integration, and a long list of fixes to the original
game. Build 03.08.26.

Posted here with his blessing.

## The English translation

**keyboard gecko.**

Almost all of the English text here came from their translation of Olympus,
not from this project. They did the overwhelming majority of the work; what
this repository adds is the port onto the Fixed Edition, 98 strings for
content the FE added, and the interface art.

Credited by name thanks to the English UI patch's own README, which finally
answered a question this file carried unanswered through several releases.

## Engine

**sfall** — the *Fallout 2* engine extender. Timeslip, NovaRain, phobos2077 and
many contributors.

**SF-Configurator**, the **High Resolution Patch**, the inventory filter and
MapBlurFX, as bundled by the Fixed Edition.

## Fallout 2

**Black Isle Studios / Interplay.** Twenty-five years on, people are still
building new games on their engine. That is the reason this scene exists.

## The community

**No Mutants Allowed** and the wider Fallout modding scene, who host, translate,
test and keep these games findable. **And countless others** — the testers, bug
reporters and forum posters who answered a question once years ago and made
somebody's build work. Most of that work is uncredited by nature.
